Before anything else in a restoration project can work, the water has to be extracted. Structural materials, floors, possessions and building systems all take less damage when standing water comes out sooner. Left standing, water keeps moving deeper by the hour, wicking into drywall, soaking carpet pad, seeping under flooring and saturating the framing. Troy Restoration provides emergency water extraction throughout Troy using industrial-grade truck-mounted and portable equipment capable of removing hundreds of gallons per minute.

Few water removal systems match the power of a truck-mounted extractor, which draws water from structural materials, carpeting and hard flooring far better than rental or consumer machines. For areas truck-mounted hoses cannot reach, we deploy powerful portable extractors, submersible pumps and specialty tools designed for specific situations, including water trapped in wall cavities, between floor layers and in confined spaces.

There is more to a professional extraction than getting rid of the water in plain sight. Water migrates behind baseboards, below flooring, into wall cavities and under cabinets, and our technicians, who are IICRC-certified, locate it with moisture detection tools. Specialty extraction tools remove as much moisture as possible from these hidden areas before the drying phase begins, which significantly reduces the time and equipment needed for structural drying and lowers the risk of secondary mold growth.

Signs It Is Time for Water Extraction

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Any room where water has collected on the floor
  • A utility area, mechanical room or crawl space with water in it
  • Water entering through a flooded basement stairwell or window well
  • Sprinkler discharge that has left water on floors and contents
  • Active leaks from plumbing, appliances or HVAC systems
  • Pooled water on the carpet, or a soggy feel with every step
  • A basement taking on water after heavy rain or a sump pump failure

Water Extraction Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Save (682) 281-8295 in your contacts now, and you can reach us at once when something floods
  • Know where your main water shutoff valve is and label it clearly
  • Test your sump pump before the rainy season so you know it will run
  • Install automatic water shutoff valves that detect leaks and stop the flow
  • Worn or aging hoses on washers, dishwashers and ice makers should be replaced before they fail
  • Keep a basic emergency water kit: old towels, a bucket and your restoration company's number

Why Professional Water Extraction Matters

The speed and thoroughness of water extraction directly determine the extent of damage to your property and the cost of restoration. Truck-mounted extractors move water at hundreds of times the rate of consumer tools, and a job that could cost you a full day with a shop vac is done by our machines in minutes. That gap in speed is more than a convenience, because each hour water touches building materials can add thousands of dollars in further damage.

Hidden moisture, the kind consumer tools never reach, is part of a professional extraction too. It follows plumbing lines and electrical conduits, slips between flooring layers, climbs drywall by wicking and is drawn into carpet pad through capillary action. Our specialty extraction tools and techniques target these hidden moisture deposits, removing significantly more water than surface extraction alone. The payoff is a shorter drying period, lower equipment cost and, most important of all, less risk of mold growing unseen in trapped moisture.
