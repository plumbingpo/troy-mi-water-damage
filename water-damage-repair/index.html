How to Prevent Water Damage Repair Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Before a contractor closes a floor or wall, request the final moisture numbers.
  • Use moisture resistant drywall and backer board when rebuilding bathrooms, kitchens and laundry rooms.
  • Completed-repair photos and the scope document belong in your home records.
  • Rebuilt bathroom walls last longer when the exhaust fan runs through and after each shower.
  • Hold off on heavy traffic and wet cleaning until new adhesives, caulk and paint have cured.
  • In case the insurance company asks down the road, photograph anything damaged while it is still in place.

Signs You Need Water Damage Repair in Troy

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • A leak overhead left the ceiling drywall cracked, sagging or falling apart.
  • Flooring flexes more than it should in the spot that was flooded.
  • Where water ran down a wall, the paint has blistered, peeled or turned brown.
  • During drying, sections of wallboard came out and the framing is still exposed.
  • Tiles sound hollow, have come loose or show cracked grout since water got beneath them.
  • Door casings and baseboards have warped or pulled off the wall surface.
  • Wood floor boards stayed raised, cupped or crowned even once the moisture was gone.

What a Professional Brings to Water Damage Repair

Rebuilding after a water loss differs from a normal remodel. Whoever rebuilds has to understand where the water travelled, what it affected and whether the framing inside the wall has fully dried. We build from the moisture records made during drying, which tell us the studs that got wet, the subfloor that was treated and the spots needing a second look. That history keeps problems from being covered over with new drywall and fresh paint.

Matching is the other skill. If the patch stands out, it reminds you of the damage daily. We blend wall texture, feather paint to natural break points and source flooring that lines up with the existing boards where it can be found. When a match is not possible, we lay out options, such as replacing flooring to a doorway. We also handle permits where the work requires them and keep your insurer updated.
