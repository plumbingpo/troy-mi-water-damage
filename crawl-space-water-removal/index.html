Crawl Space Water Removal: What Troy Homeowners Should Know

A flooded crawl space rarely announces itself. It tends to turn up as a damp chill underfoot, a stale smell at the vents, or a small lake discovered the day somebody lifts the hatch. By then the insulation is usually soaked and the wood above it has been wet for days. Crews from our company extract the water, haul away anything that cannot be saved and dry the structure ahead of rot.

Once the standing water is gone, the job is only halfway done. Wood framing takes water in gradually and gives it up just as gradually, which is why we meter the joists, beams and subfloor and leave dehumidifiers on until readings drop, typically 3 to 5 days. Our team traces the source too, whether that is a supply line leak, a drain, or rain driven groundwater, so the space stays dry afterward.

Warning Signs

  • Dark water marks and chalky deposits partway up the block or concrete
  • Puddles or wet muck visible just inside the crawl space door
  • Ducts and plumbing lines below the house covered in condensation
  • An increase in insects and pests that like damp, dark spaces
  • Stale, mildew type air coming up around the vents in the floor
  • Joists, beams or subfloor panels coated with whitish or blackish fuzz
The Clock

The Damage Timeline

First day

Water Pools Below

Water collects in the low spots and soaks into the soil. Batts begin drawing moisture upward and the air below the house turns humid quickly.

24 to 48 hours

Mold Gets Started

Mold can start on damp joists and paper faced insulation. Soaked insulation gains weight and starts to sag off the floor above.

Day 3 to 7

Dampness Rises Indoors

The moisture starts reaching the part of the house you live in. You might see floorboards cupping and notice a musty odor while the framing continues to soak.

After a week

Wood Starts Rotting

Lumber left damp this long starts breaking down. Expect softer framing, rusted fasteners, and a wet environment that draws in termites and similar pests.

Hidden Risks

What Hides Behind It

What You Breathe Upstairs

Because air naturally moves upward from under the home, spores and stale odors in a wet crawl space reach your living areas too.

Contaminated Water

Do not assume the water below your floor is clean. If it came from a drain pipe or from waste laden groundwater it is rated Category 2 or 3, so we suit up and disinfect.

Electrical and Duct Hazards

Electrical runs, junction boxes and ductwork pass through most crawl spaces. Wires under water can shock someone, and wet duct wrap pushes a moldy smell through every vent.

Rot You Cannot See

The first soft wood is usually hidden, up where joists meet the subfloor or back at the sill plate. We probe the wood to find soft spots.

Our Process

How We Handle Crawl Space Water Removal

1

Inspect and Make Safe

Nobody enters until the space has been screened for shock risks, hanging ducts and contamination. After that our team marks out the wet area and records how much moisture the framing and subfloor hold.

2

Remove Standing Water

We run pumps and extraction lines until the pooled water is gone, low pockets and trenches along the footings included. Mud and silt left on the liner are cleared at the same time.

3

Clear Out Ruined Material

We bag up the wet insulation, damaged vapor barrier and loose debris and take it all outside. Our crew then wipes down the uncovered framing and applies antimicrobial to spots with growth or a high chance of it.

4

Dry and Verify

A dehumidifier and low profile air movers stay running beneath the floor until the lumber meters at a normal level. We recheck with a meter and give you the readings and a prevention plan.
