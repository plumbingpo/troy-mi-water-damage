Mold is more than an unsightly nuisance: it is a serious health hazard and a sign of underlying moisture problems that threaten the structure of your property. Children, older adults and anyone with a weakened immune system are hit hardest by exposure, which can bring headaches, allergic reactions, breathing trouble and worse. Troy Restoration sends IICRC-certified technicians to homes and commercial buildings across Troy, where they contain mold, remove it and keep it from returning under strict industry protocols.

Real remediation involves a great deal more than cleaning visible growth from a wall. A professional job has five parts: locate the water source that feeds the growth, build proper containment against cross-contamination, use HEPA-filtered tools to remove damaged materials safely, treat surfaces left behind with an antimicrobial solution, and resolve the moisture problem at its root to stop recurrence. Working this way removes the mold where it starts instead of covering it up temporarily.

Each project starts with a careful inspection, and in many cases we test the air to learn which mold spores are present and at what concentration. With that data we can build a remediation plan aimed at exactly what your property needs. Throughout the project we maintain negative air pressure containment, run HEPA air scrubbers and follow the IICRC S520 standard for mold remediation. Post-remediation verification, including clearance air testing when requested, confirms that your property meets safe occupancy standards.

Why Hire a Professional for Mold Remediation?

Removing mold is work homeowners should not attempt on their own. Disturbing mold colonies without proper containment sends millions of spores airborne, which can contaminate unaffected areas of your home and create worse health exposure than the original problem. Our crews work in personal protective equipment inside a negative-pressure enclosure, with HEPA air scrubbers running, to trap spores and hold them in the work area while removal is underway.

Just as important, we deal with moisture, the underlying cause of every mold problem. Removing visible mold without correcting the underlying moisture problem means the mold will return, often within weeks. Our certified technicians are trained and equipped to track down moisture you cannot see, dry the affected areas the right way and make the corrections that keep mold from recurring.

Signs It Is Time for Mold Remediation

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Fuzzy or spotted growth on stored boxes, clothing or the backs of furniture
  • Feeling better away from home, then having coughs, headaches or allergies come back indoors
  • Patches of mold, whatever the color, on a ceiling, wall, floor or other surface
  • Warping, blistered paint or wallpaper lifting off the wall, all clues to moisture you cannot see
  • Discolored or darkened spots near HVAC vents, across ceilings or on walls
  • Discovering a plumbing or roof leak that has been dripping for a long time
  • An earthy, musty smell that will not go away, mostly in humid or closed-off spaces

Maintenance Tips for Mold Remediation

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Mold-resistant paint and wallboard are the better choice wherever moisture is common, such as a basement or bath
  • Aim for 30 to 50% indoor humidity and never let it stay above 60%, running dehumidifiers and ventilating as needed
  • Regular filter changes and HVAC maintenance help keep mold out of the ducts
  • Hang or launder wet fabrics promptly and wipe up spills as they happen
  • Check that exhaust fans in the kitchen, laundry room and bathrooms move enough air
  • Overflowing gutters send water into basements and crawl spaces, so clean them on a schedule
